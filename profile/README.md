### Helloblue Research Lab

We're an independent, open-source research and engineering hub dedicated to advancing Natural Language Processing (NLP), machine learning, AI reasoning, and human-centered intelligent systems.

As a member of the **NVIDIA Inception Program**, Helloblue benefits from access to technical resources, industry expertise, and an innovation ecosystem designed to support organizations developing advanced AI technologies.

<img width="300" alt="NVIDIA Inception Program badge" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/73fcd70d-4460-44cd-851d-a6da2781bffa" />


We build adaptive, context-aware AI that improves communication, expands accessibility, and evolves alongside human needs. Our goal is to bridge the gap between theoretical breakthroughs and practical applications, ensuring that our research produces meaningful, real-world impact.

### Open-Source Ecosystem

Our open-source projects including **Helloblue GK**, **Bleu.js**, and the **Bleu.js Reasoning Lab** provide a collaborative environment where developers, researchers, university students, recent graduates, and independent contributors can explore ideas and help shape the future of AI.

Through these repositories, contributors can:

- Participate in applied AI and machine-learning research.
- Experiment with NLP, reasoning systems, and AI orchestration.
- Build new tools, integrations, benchmarks, and prototypes.
- Improve documentation, testing, accessibility, and developer experience.
- Gain practical experience by contributing to real-world projects.
- Collaborate with an international community of engineers and researchers.
- Turn academic knowledge into practical, production-oriented solutions.
